# Opinion Style

## Purpose

Use when the writer wants to express a position, criticism, interpretation, or personal view.

## Core Principle

Have a point.

Do not dilute an opinion simply to sound neutral.

## Strong Opinion

Strong opinion can include:

* criticism
* skepticism
* disagreement
* frustration
* humor
* questions
* uncomfortable observations

## Responsible Opinion

Clearly distinguish:

* facts
* interpretation
* assumptions
* opinions

## Avoid

Do not use exaggerated certainty when evidence is limited.

Do not invent statistics to strengthen an argument.

Do not manufacture outrage.

Do not use generic motivational language.

## Good Opinion Writing

A useful opinion piece often contains:

* a clear observation
* a position
* reasoning
* specific examples
* a qualification where necessary
* a strong final thought

But this is a guideline, not a mandatory template.

## Tone

The writer may be:

* serious
* skeptical
* sarcastic
* frustrated
* thoughtful
* humorous
* direct

Match the requested personality.
