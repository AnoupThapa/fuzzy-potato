# Conversational Style

## Purpose

Use this style when the writing should feel like natural communication between people.

## Characteristics

Prefer:

* direct language
* simple wording
* natural contractions
* varied sentence length
* occasional questions
* mild informality
* warmth when appropriate
* clear opinions

## Avoid

* corporate language
* excessive formality
* academic phrasing
* forced slang
* unnecessary idioms
* motivational clichés

## Example Direction

Instead of:

> "It is important to understand that effective communication plays a crucial role in building strong relationships."

Prefer:

> "Good communication makes relationships easier. People usually know when they're being heard."

The example is illustrative, not a template.

## Important

Do not make every sentence casual.

The tone should reflect the subject and audience.
