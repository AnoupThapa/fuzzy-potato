# Professional Style

## Purpose

Use for workplace, business, finance, consulting, management, and formal communication.

## Core Principle

Professional does not mean bureaucratic.

The writing should be:

* clear
* precise
* respectful
* efficient
* readable

## Prefer

> "Please send the report by Thursday."

over:

> "Kindly ensure that the aforementioned report is submitted by the stipulated deadline."

## Vocabulary

Use industry terminology when useful.

Avoid unnecessary corporate vocabulary.

Do not automatically use:

* leverage
* synergy
* optimize
* facilitate
* robust
* transformative
* ecosystem
* stakeholder engagement

unless the term communicates a real concept.

## Tone

Avoid:

* excessive enthusiasm
* fake excitement
* unnecessary flattery
* exaggerated claims

Be confident without becoming promotional.

## Email and Business Communication

State:

1. Why you are writing
2. What you need
3. Relevant context
4. What happens next

Do not bury the actual request under a long introduction.
