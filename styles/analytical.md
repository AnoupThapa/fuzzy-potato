# Analytical Style

## Purpose

Use when explaining, evaluating, comparing, or reasoning about a subject.

## Priorities

Focus on:

* claims
* evidence
* reasoning
* cause and effect
* distinctions
* limitations
* implications
* uncertainty

## Structure

Choose the structure appropriate to the argument.

Possible structure:

```text
Question
↓
Evidence
↓
Interpretation
↓
Counterpoint or limitation
↓
Conclusion
```

But do not force this structure.

## Evidence

Do not invent supporting evidence.

If evidence is unavailable, say so.

## Balance

Do not manufacture artificial balance.

If the evidence strongly supports one conclusion, state that clearly.

If evidence is mixed, explain why.

## Language

Analytical writing should be precise without becoming unnecessarily academic.

Avoid complicated vocabulary when ordinary language is more precise.

## Distinguish

Keep separate:

* what happened
* why it happened
* what it means
* what you think about it

This prevents opinion from being presented as fact.
