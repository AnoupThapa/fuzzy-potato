# Draft Workflow

## Step 1 — Identify Intent

Determine what the user wants to accomplish.

Possible goals:

* inform
* explain
* persuade
* criticize
* entertain
* request
* announce
* reflect
* sell
* teach

## Step 2 — Identify Audience

Determine who will read it.

## Step 3 — Identify Tone

Determine whether it should be:

* formal
* conversational
* professional
* humorous
* analytical
* critical
* persuasive
* neutral

## Step 4 — Extract Facts

Separate known facts from opinions and assumptions.

## Step 5 — Find the Central Thought

Identify the one idea the reader should remember.

## Step 6 — Select Structure

Choose a structure that fits the content.

Do not use a template automatically.

## Step 7 — Draft

Write naturally.

Do not optimize for length.

## Step 8 — Apply Style

Apply the relevant style module.

## Step 9 — Apply Platform Rules

If applicable, apply:

* Facebook
* LinkedIn
* Instagram
* X
* Email

## Step 10 — Final Edit

Apply `rules/final-edit.md`.

## Step 11 — Output

Return the finished content unless the user requested an explanation or alternatives.
