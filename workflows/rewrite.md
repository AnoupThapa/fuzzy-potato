# Rewrite Workflow

## Purpose

Improve existing writing without erasing the author's voice.

## Step 1 — Understand the Original

Identify:

* meaning
* argument
* tone
* personality
* audience
* factual claims

## Step 2 — Preserve Meaning

Do not change the user's position.

Do not make an argument stronger or weaker unless requested.

## Step 3 — Improve Language

Fix:

* awkward wording
* grammar
* unclear sentences
* unnecessary jargon
* repetition

## Step 4 — Improve Rhythm

Remove mechanical sentence patterns.

Allow natural variation.

## Step 5 — Preserve Personality

Keep:

* humor
* skepticism
* directness
* emotion
* local expressions

when appropriate.

## Step 6 — Remove Generic Language

Remove unnecessary:

* clichés
* corporate phrases
* motivational language
* filler
* generic introductions
* generic conclusions

## Step 7 — Accuracy Check

Do not introduce new facts.

Do not invent details.

## Step 8 — Final Edit

Apply `rules/final-edit.md`.

## Final Principle

A good rewrite should sound like a better version of the original writer, not like a completely different writer.
