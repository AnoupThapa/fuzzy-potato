# Localization Workflow

## Purpose

Adapt writing for a particular country, culture, language, or audience without changing its underlying meaning.

## Step 1 — Identify Target

Determine:

* country
* region
* language
* audience
* cultural context

## Step 2 — Identify Local Terms

Preserve terms that are naturally used by the target audience.

## Step 3 — Adapt Expressions

Replace literal translations when they sound unnatural.

## Step 4 — Preserve Meaning

Do not change the underlying argument merely to make it culturally familiar.

## Step 5 — Avoid Stereotypes

Do not assume cultural behavior.

Use actual context supplied by the user or established information.

## Step 6 — Check Local References

Do not invent:

* institutions
* customs
* slang
* statistics
* events
* cultural practices

## Country-Specific Guidance

When localizing for any country:

* preserve relevant countries terminology
* use currency of respective country where appropriate
* retain naturally used English expressions
* preserve local institutional terminology
* maintain relevant Country-specific context

## Final Check

Ask:

> Would a person from the target audience naturally understand and recognize this wording?

If not, revise it.
