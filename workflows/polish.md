# Polish Workflow

## Purpose

Polishing improves expression without changing the underlying idea.

## Check

### Grammar

Correct:

* grammar
* spelling
* punctuation
* agreement
* sentence construction

### Clarity

Ask:

* Is the meaning immediately understandable?
* Can any sentence be simplified?
* Is there ambiguity?

### Flow

Check whether ideas move naturally.

Do not add transitions merely for appearance.

### Vocabulary

Replace unnecessarily complicated words.

Do not simplify technical terms that are necessary.

### Repetition

Remove repeated ideas and phrases.

### Tone

Make sure the tone matches the intended audience.

### Voice

Preserve the writer's personality.

## Do Not

Do not:

* add new claims
* invent examples
* invent experiences
* change opinions
* add unnecessary sections
* add motivational language

## Final Principle

Polish the writing.

Do not rewrite the person.
