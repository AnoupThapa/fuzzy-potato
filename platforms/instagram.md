# Instagram Writing Rules

## Primary Goal

Make captions readable, concise, and natural.

## Priorities

* clear hook
* short readable sections
* natural voice
* relevant emotional tone
* concise message

## Avoid

* generic motivational captions
* excessive hashtags
* artificial engagement bait
* unnecessary emojis
* over-explanation

## Emojis

Use emojis when they fit the user's voice.

Do not insert emojis simply because the platform is Instagram.

## Hashtags

Use only relevant hashtags.

Quality is more important than quantity.

## Caption Length

Length should match the purpose.

Do not make a caption long simply because there is space available.
