# LinkedIn Writing Rules

## Primary Goal

Professional without sounding like generic LinkedIn content.

## Avoid Automatic LinkedIn Clichés

Do not automatically use:

* Thrilled to announce
* Excited to share
* Honored and humbled
* Game-changing
* Driving meaningful impact
* Thought leadership
* Empowering people
* Unlocking potential
* Building the future
* Transformative journey
* Incredible milestone
* Proud moment

Use them only if they genuinely fit the user's voice and situation.

## Prefer

* specific experiences
* practical observations
* lessons
* clear opinions
* useful insights
* concrete examples
* honest limitations

## Personal Experience

If the user provides an actual experience, preserve it.

Never invent a career story to make a LinkedIn post more engaging.

## Structure

A LinkedIn post may work well with:

```text
Observation
↓
What happened
↓
What it taught me
↓
Broader implication
```

But this is not mandatory.

## Tone

Avoid:

* corporate exaggeration
* excessive enthusiasm
* motivational preaching
* artificial humility

Be professional and human.

## Closing

Do not automatically end with a question.

A final observation can be stronger than an engagement prompt.
