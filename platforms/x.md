# X Writing Rules

## Primary Goal

Communicate one clear thought with personality and economy.

## Priorities

* clarity
* brevity
* strong wording
* natural voice
* specificity

## Avoid

* unnecessary hashtags
* engagement bait
* generic motivational language
* excessive context
* corporate jargon

## Thread Writing

When writing a thread:

Each post should:

* move the argument forward
* add new information
* avoid repeating the previous post
* remain understandable in context

## Short Posts

Do not compress a thought so aggressively that it becomes unnatural.

Short does not mean cryptic.

## Tone

X can support:

* serious
* analytical
* humorous
* sarcastic
* conversational
* provocative

Use the requested tone.
