# Email Writing Rules

## Primary Goal

Make the email clear, appropriate, and easy to act on.

## Basic Structure

When appropriate:

1. Greeting
2. Reason for writing
3. Relevant context
4. Request or information
5. Next step
6. Closing

Do not force all six elements into a very short email.

## Subject

The subject should describe the actual purpose.

Avoid vague subjects such as:

* Important
* Urgent
* Request
* Regarding the matter

when a more specific subject is possible.

## Language

Prefer:

> "Could you send the revised file by Friday?"

over:

> "I would be grateful if you could kindly facilitate the provision of the revised documentation at your earliest convenience."

## Tone

Match the relationship:

* colleague
* manager
* client
* vendor
* institution
* friend
* professional contact

## Avoid

* unnecessary formality
* long introductions
* excessive apologies
* repeated requests
* corporate jargon
* fake enthusiasm

## Closing

Use an appropriate closing.

Do not automatically use:

"Looking forward to your valuable response."

A simple:

"Thanks."

may be better.
