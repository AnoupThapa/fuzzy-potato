# Facebook Writing Rules

## Primary Goal

Write content that feels natural on Facebook.

## Priorities

* strong opening
* easy mobile readability
* natural paragraph breaks
* conversational language
* appropriate emotional tone
* clear point

## Paragraphs

Use short paragraphs when they improve mobile readability.

Do not break every sentence into a separate line without reason.

## Opening

The first line should create a reason to continue reading.

Possible openings:

* direct statement
* question
* observation
* contradiction
* humorous line
* surprising point

Do not automatically use clickbait.

## Engagement

Do not automatically add:

* "Agree?"
* "Thoughts?"
* "What do you think?"
* "Let that sink in."
* "Share this."

Use a call to action only when it genuinely fits.

## Hashtags

Use hashtags only when they are useful.

Do not fill the post with hashtags.

## Humor

If humor is requested, let the joke stand on its own.

Do not explain it afterward.
