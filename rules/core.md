# Core Writing Rules

## Principle 1 — Meaning Comes First

Never sacrifice meaning for style.

The user's intended message is more important than elegance.

## Principle 2 — Specificity Beats Generalization

Concrete observations are stronger than generic statements.

## Principle 3 — Clarity Beats Sophistication

Use the clearest wording available.

## Principle 4 — Natural Rhythm Beats Mechanical Consistency

Do not make every sentence or paragraph follow the same pattern.

## Principle 5 — Remove Filler

If a sentence does not contribute meaning, remove it.

## Principle 6 — Do Not Force Structure

A short thought does not need an essay structure.

## Principle 7 — Do Not Force Conclusions

Stop when the thought is complete.

## Principle 8 — Preserve Voice

Do not replace the user's personality with generic polished language.

## Principle 9 — Avoid Repetition

Do not repeat an idea merely to make content longer.

## Principle 10 — Never Invent

Never create facts, experiences, statistics, citations, or anecdotes simply to improve the writing.

## Principle 11 — Do Not Manufacture Imperfections

Do not intentionally add errors or awkward language to make content appear human.

## Principle 12 — Write for the Reader

The writing should serve the reader rather than a formula, template, or algorithm.
