# Final Edit Checklist

Before returning the final text, silently perform this review.

## 1. Meaning

* Did I preserve the user's intended message?
* Did I change the strength of their argument?
* Did I accidentally introduce a new position?

## 2. Language

* Are any words unnecessarily sophisticated?
* Can any sentence be said more simply?
* Did I use jargon without a reason?
* Did I overuse buzzwords?

## 3. Structure

* Is the structure appropriate for the task?
* Did I force an introduction?
* Did I force a conclusion?
* Are headings actually useful?
* Are paragraphs too uniform?

## 4. Rhythm

* Are sentences unnaturally similar?
* Is every sentence long?
* Is every sentence short?
* Did I overuse transition words?

Natural variation is good.

Artificial variation is not.

## 5. Voice

* Does the tone fit the intended audience?
* Does the writing retain personality?
* Did I accidentally turn it into generic corporate language?
* Did I make it unnecessarily formal?

## 6. Specificity

* Are important points concrete?
* Did I use vague language where the source provides useful detail?
* Did I invent details to make the writing more specific?

## 7. Repetition

Remove repeated:

* ideas
* sentences
* adjectives
* transition words
* conclusions
* phrases

## 8. Filler

Delete sentences whose primary purpose is to make the text longer.

## 9. Accuracy

Ask:

* Did I invent anything?
* Did I make unsupported factual claims?
* Did I accidentally present opinion as fact?

## 10. Final Read

Read the piece mentally as a reader rather than as an editor.

Ask:

> "Would a real person naturally communicate this idea this way?"

If not, revise the wording.

## Final Removal Pass

Remove anything that exists only to make the writing:

* longer
* more impressive
* more corporate
* more academic
* more formulaic

The final version should be clean without being sterile.
